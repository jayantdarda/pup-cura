Care,
as it happens.

topic

Training

From the first sit to off-leash reliability.

Small daily reps compound into deep trust. Five-minute sessions, twice a day, beat an hour once a week — every time.

Start where your dog is, not where you wish they were. Reward what you want more of. Ignore what you can.

  • Sit, Stay, Come, Heel — the four you live on
  • Positive reinforcement — reward what you want more of
  • Socialization — early exposure, low stakes
  • Common mistakes — inconsistency, punishment, skipped days

topic

Nutrition

Food is the first medicine.

Quality, consistency, and timing beat every supplement on the shelf. Read the label, watch the weight, and keep mealtimes sacred.

If your dog will eat it twice a day for the next twelve years — that's the right food.

  • Recommended food types — proteins, grains, vet-approved formulas
  • Healthy treats — carrots, apple, dental chews
  • Foods to avoid — chocolate, grapes, onions, xylitol
  • Hydration — fresh water, always
  • Feeding schedule — two meals a day for most adult dogs

topic

Health

Prevention is quieter than treatment — and cheaper.

Most of what keeps a dog well is invisible: vaccines that are current, teeth that are brushed, weight that's steady, energy that's normal.

Know your dog's baseline. Anything that shifts it — appetite, breathing, mood — is worth a phone call to your vet.

  • Vaccination — rabies, distemper, parvovirus
  • Routine vet visits — annual, kept
  • Dental care — brush, chews, watch the gums
  • Exercise — daily, varied, age-appropriate
  • Signs of illness — appetite, energy, breathing, behavior

topic

Daily care

The boring habits, repeated well.

Grooming, walking, sleep — the things that sound obvious until you skip them. Coats matt, joints stiffen, minds dull.

A short daily routine, kept for years, is what keeps a dog in good shape long past puppyhood.

  • Grooming — regular brushing, coat health
  • Bathing — only as needed; dog-specific shampoo
  • Walking — exercise, stimulation, bonding
  • Mental stimulation — puzzle toys, training games
  • Sleep — 12–14 hours for adults, quiet and dark
